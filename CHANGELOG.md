# Changelog

## 1.2.3

- En vivo follows the addon's manifest: the addon dropped its four live-sports catalogs in late October (its manifest lists none and each answers 404), so the plugin showed four empty tabs and reported errors. It now offers only the live catalogs the addon declares (none today) and treats a 404 catalog as empty. Movies and series are unaffected.

## 1.2.2

- Faster opening: a tab's kept copy is shown for up to 7 days (refreshed behind) instead of falling back to skeletons after 12 hours, and with no copy of its own a tab is drawn from the kept shelves.
